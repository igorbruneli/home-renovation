Plans, budgets and 3D models for the work on the house: what to do first, what it costs, and one row per project.

Where to start

Two projects, one order. Cheap and urgent first, then the money that is already there, and the big spend last, once it has a firm price.

  1. Stop the water and service the boiler≈ 150 € Now · November 2026 The drainage works come before everything: nothing new goes on the ground-floor walls until the pipe is fixed and the walls have dried. Book the gas boiler's yearly service in the same weeks. It is a legal duty, and the 2024 report found no certificate. Hall plan
  2. Measure the kitchen and collect two quotes0 € This winter, alongside the hall Thirty minutes with a tape measure, then a quote from an electrician and one from a gas fitter. It costs nothing and turns “somewhere between 6,300 and 10,000 €” into a number you can save for. Kitchen, K-01
  3. Build the ground floor hall≈ 1,865 € November 2026 – February 2027 The insurance payout of 1,800 € covers it and the walls are already open. It is the smaller job, so it goes first: the dust and the flood-proofing are out of the way before the kitchen starts. Ground floor hall
  4. A first slice of the kitchen: the back-wall run Optional≈ 950–1,050 € Any time after step 2 The two new base units and two wall units on the back wall replace the microwave table. They touch neither the gas, the water nor today's units, so they can go in months before the rest, in the same fronts and worktop. Only do it once the worktop choice and the wall-cabinet height are final. Kitchen, K-02
  5. Build the kitchen6,300–10,000 € From spring 2027, when the money is set aside One to three weeks without a kitchen, depending on who builds it. Doing most of it yourselves, with a fitter for the worktop cuts, is the best value at about 7,500 €. Kitchen, K-04
  • Water, gas and electrics before finishes. They are cheap to get right and expensive to get wrong.
  • Spend earmarked money first. The payout is for the hall, and repaired damage keeps the insurance file clean.
  • Decide for free before paying. Measurements and quotes cost nothing and remove most of the risk.
  • One building site at a time. The hall is finished before the kitchen starts, so only one floor is in pieces.
