Plans, budgets and 3D models for the work on the house: what to do first, what it costs, and one row per project.

Where to start

Two projects, one order. Cheap and urgent first, then the money that is already there, and the kitchen in two stages so the big spend comes last.

  1. Stop the water and service the boiler≈ 150 € Now · November 2026 The drainage works come before everything: nothing new goes on the ground-floor walls until the pipe is fixed and the walls have dried. Book the gas boiler's yearly service in the same weeks. It is a legal duty, and the 2024 report found no certificate. The forecourt in 3D, before and after · Hall plan
  2. Measure the kitchen and collect two quotes0 € This winter, alongside the hall Thirty minutes with a tape measure, then a quote from an electrician and one from a gas fitter. It costs nothing and turns the cost of stage 2 into a number you can save for. Kitchen, K-01
  3. Build the ground floor hall≈ 1,865 € November 2026 – February 2027 The insurance payout of 1,800 € covers it and the walls are already open. It is the smaller job, so it goes first: the dust and the flood-proofing are out of the way before the kitchen starts. Ground floor hall
  4. Kitchen, stage 1: the back wall and the wall cabinets≈ 2,660 € Order by 16 November · two weekends of work You build it yourselves: two base and two wall cabinets with a worktop on the back wall, then four wall cabinets on the side wall. The hood is bought now and goes up with the induction hob. The sink stays in use throughout. Ordered before 22 November with IKEA Family, about 210 € comes back as a voucher. The page checks your cart line by line and walks you through every step. Kitchen, S-1
  5. Kitchen, stage 2: base cabinets, worktops, hob and sink≈ 4,700 € From spring 2027, when the money is set aside The part that needs the electrician and the gas fitter, and about two weeks without a hob. The last wall cabinet and the hood go up then too. Kitchen, K-04
  • Water, gas and electrics before finishes. They are cheap to get right and expensive to get wrong.
  • Spend earmarked money first. The payout is for the hall, and repaired damage keeps the insurance file clean.
  • Decide for free before paying. Measurements and quotes cost nothing and remove most of the risk.
  • One building site at a time. The hall is finished before the kitchen starts, so only one floor is in pieces.
