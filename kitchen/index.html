How to order

“One line per article” is the list to order from: it adds up the same article across cabinets. The dashed number is IKEA's article number; click it to copy, then paste it into the search box on ikea.com. “Copy order list” puts the whole list on the clipboard, ready to email to IKEA's kitchen service or to check off at the store.

Prices and stock were read from ikea.com/fr on . IKEA changes them without notice, so the product page is the reference. Kitchen orders over a set amount often qualify for IKEA's kitchen offers: ask before paying.
