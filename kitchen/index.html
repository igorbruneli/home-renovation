K-03

What I'd change or check in his plan

The layout is sound. The gaps are in the list, in the work around it, and in a few things nobody measured. Red = fix before ordering, amber = work to budget for, blue = check on site, green = ways to spend less.

Fix before you order

406 € of parts are not in the printed total List

The list has no door hinges at all: eleven hinged doors need two each. The planner wrote in by hand that the corner unit needs hinges, the hood needs a filter and a duct, two side panels must be off-white, and the worktop and niche need brackets and three more LED strips. None of that is priced.

Done here: all of it is in the shopping list under “Added by the planner” (306 €) and “Missing from the list” (100 €).

The quotes have run out and prices moved Quotes

All three are dated 9 December 2025 and valid for three months. Since then 33 lines of the list changed price, +106 € in total: the hob is up 70 €, the tap up 10 €, the hood down 20 €.

If IKEA fits: ask for both quotes again, against the corrected list. The price per metre of fitting may have moved too.

The dimmer no longer exists List

The three RODRET dimmers are discontinued. IKEA's current remote, BILRESA, uses a newer radio standard; IKEA's pages say it works with these LED drivers through the DIRIGERA hub, and buyers report that pairing the two directly fails.

Cheapest: have the electrician put the LED feeds on a wall switch. On and off, no dimming, nothing to pair. 0 € of IKEA parts. With dimming: DIRIGERA hub plus one BILRESA remote. Ask IKEA first whether any remote still pairs without the hub.

The plinth is a different white List

The plinth on the list is high-gloss white. Fronts and side panels are off-white, so the strip along the floor would show as a colder, shinier white.

Swap: FÖRBÄTTRA plinth in off-white, same price. It is the default under “Plinth colour” in the list.

Work nobody has quoted

Gas to induction is the biggest hidden job ≈ 530–1,050 €

The induction hob needs its own 32 A circuit in 6 mm² cable from the consumer unit up to the kitchen, and the gas supply to the old hob has to be disconnected and capped by a gas professional. The technical report lists both as the client's work.

As planned: electrician ≈ 450–900 € for the hob line and the other outlets, gas fitter ≈ 80–150 €. Estimates: get quotes. Alternative, stay on gas: a LAGAN gas hob fits the same cabinet. No new line, no capping, and 590 € less for the hob. You keep an open flame under timber beams and a recirculating hood, and the hood must sit higher above a gas hob than the 50 cm drawn, so check its manual before choosing this.

Bare walls after the old kitchen comes out ≈ 230–450 €

The removal quote says it in so many words: taking off the old wall tiles and repairing the surfaces is not included. And no quote has a splashback, so behind the hob and the worktop there would be painted plaster.

Walls: tiles off, filler, primer, two coats. About 80 € of materials if you do it, ≈ 300 € if you pay for it. Splashback, about 2 m²: IKEA wall panels ↗ from 45 € a panel plus fixing strips, tiles, or a scrubbable kitchen paint for about 40 €.

VAT at 20% on both labour quotes ≈ 207 € to ask about

In a home more than two years old, fitting labour can usually be invoiced at 10% VAT once you sign the attestation. IKEA's fitting quote even has a line for it, “Droit à la TVA 10%”, yet both quotes are calculated at 20%.

Ask: “Can you apply the 10% rate with the attestation simplifiée?” At 10% the fitting quote drops by 129 € and the annex works by 79 €. The goods stay at 20%.

Check on site first

Wall cabinets against the beams Measure

The drawings assume a flat ceiling at 2.50 m. The wall cabinets top out at 2.22 m on the side wall. The two on the back wall are drawn 8 cm higher, at 2.30 m, with 58 cm above the worktop instead of 50. Your beams hang below the ceiling.

If a beam is in the way: hang all of them at 2.22 m, or use METOD wall cabinets 60 cm high on that wall. The 3D model shows them all at 2.22 m.

The hood is loud when it recirculates Decide

IKEA gives 630 m³/h at 64 dB for the UNDERVERK when it blows outside, and 365 m³/h at 79 dB when it recirculates through a charcoal filter, both at full speed. Blowing outside means a 150 mm hole through the wall, and only works if that wall is an outside wall.

Keep it: cook on speed 1 or 2 and accept the noise on full. Alternative: the UTDRAG pull-out hood recirculates at 72 dB but moves only 140 m³/h. It mounts differently, so the wall cabinet above the hob changes with it: ask IKEA to swap both.

Getting the worktops up the stairs Measure

The box for this is unticked in the technical report. Each slab is 2.46 m long, 64 cm wide and heavy.

If the turn is tight: cut the slabs to length in the garage first. The longest finished piece is the peninsula, 2.12 m.

No oven on the list Confirm

The list has the oven cabinet and its support, and the fitting quote counts four appliances, so the plan re-uses your oven and dishwasher. That works if the oven is a standard 60 cm built-in model.

If you'd rather replace it: LAGAN from 199 €, STENABY 279 €. Pick one under “Oven” in the list and it is added to the total.

Sockets for the new run Ask

The report places outlets for the hob, oven, hood, dishwasher and LED drivers. It shows none above the new worktop on the back wall, where the microwave and small appliances will live.

Ask the electrician for a double socket above that worktop, and whether the “one socket moved” in the annex quote is the one that would end up behind the new units.

Boxes left unticked in the report Fitter

Whether the walls are square, the state of the walls, the corner door next to the hood cabinet and the filler of the corner base, and the switches and door swings near the units. Whoever fits the kitchen should confirm these before you order.

Where the plan can cost less

Laminate instead of oak veneer −396 €

Oak veneer is real wood on a core: it looks best, but it needs oiling and the sink cut-out has to be sealed perfectly. SÄLJAN oak-effect laminate is the same thickness, needs no care, and costs less than half. The niche shelves then get the iron-on edging on their cut ends.

A simpler hob −220 to −480 €

The HÖGKLASSIG went up to 699 €. Any 59 cm IKEA induction hob fits the same cabinet and needs the same 32 A line. The choices are in the list.

Do the removal and the shelves yourselves −573 €

Taking out the old kitchen is priced at 396 € and cutting three shelves from the spare worktop at 177 €. Both are a day's work with a screwdriver, a pry bar and a circular saw, once the gas and power to the hob are safely off.

Trim the lighting −105 to −220 €

Lighting is about 360 € of parts, plus 266 € of labour in the annex quote. The strips under the wall cabinets earn their keep. The niche lighting is the part to drop: three strips, a driver and a cable are about 105 € of parts and 112 € of labour.

Shelves in the corner instead of the pull-out ≈ −130 €

The pull-out fitting for the corner base costs 149 €. The same cabinet with plain shelves stores as much; you just reach in for it. Ask IKEA for the corner base with shelves.

If the money comes in stages

The new run on the back wall, cabinets 2, 3, 11 and 12 with one worktop slab, panels and hinges, comes to about 950–1,050 €. It needs no gas, water or new circuit, so it can go in first and the rest later. Settle the worktop and the wall-cabinet height before buying it.

In every route

The electrician and the gas fitter are not optional and not DIY. A hob circuit done wrong is a fire risk your insurer can refuse to cover, and gas work is reserved for a qualified professional.

Tools for the DIY routes
  • Cordless drill-driver, and a hammer drill with masonry bits for the stone walls.
  • Anchors made for stone or hollow masonry for the suspension rail. The plugs in the box are for plain concrete.
  • A long spirit level or a cross-line laser, tape, pencil, clamps.
  • For the worktops: a plunge or circular saw with a fine blade and a guide rail, a jigsaw for the cut-outs, worktop connecting bolts, clear silicone, hard-wax oil.
  • A handle-drilling jig (IKEA's FIXA template is out of stock online; any DIY store has one).
  • A second pair of hands for the wall cabinets and every worktop.

Hire shops rent a plunge saw with rail for about 30–40 € a day.

Say this to the electrician
  • « Une ligne dédiée 32 A en 6 mm² pour une plaque à induction, du tableau jusqu'à la cuisine au 1er étage. »
  • « Une sortie de câble 20 A pour le four, une prise 16 A pour la hotte à 2 m, une prise 20 A pour le lave-vaisselle sous l'évier. »
  • « Des alimentations pour l'éclairage LED derrière les meubles hauts, commandées par un interrupteur. »
  • « Une prise double au-dessus du nouveau plan de travail. »

Ask whether the consumer unit has room, and whether the hob circuit will sit behind a type A 30 mA RCD as the French wiring rules ask. Your 2024 report found the installation in good order, which keeps this simple.

Say this to the gas fitter
  • « Dépose de la plaque de cuisson gaz et bouchonnage de l'arrivée, par un professionnel du gaz. »

The boiler in the garage stays on gas, so only the branch to the hob is capped. It's a good moment to book the boiler's yearly service with the same visit.

Say this to a fitter
  • « Pose d'une cuisine IKEA METOD : 14 caissons, 4 plans de travail en chêne plaqué avec découpes pour l'évier et la plaque, raccordement de l'évier et du lave-vaisselle. »
  • « Dépose et évacuation de l'ancienne cuisine. Prix forfaitaire, TVA à 10 % avec attestation. »

Send them the plan in K-02 and the list in K-07 so the price is for this kitchen, not a guess.
